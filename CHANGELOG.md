## 1.0.0

- Initial release.
- `LiquidGlassToast.show` with `success`, `error`, `info` and `warning` types.
- `LiquidGlassToastScope` for app-wide theming (colors, icons, text styles,
  durations, positions and pixel spacing/sizing).
- Swipe-to-dismiss, auto duration based on message length, top/bottom positions.
